SECOND BRAIN GD

Your knowledge. Your system. Your control.

A personal knowledge environment that runs on your own computer, with an assistant who briefs you each morning, answers from your own notes and files every thought you type or say. Built with a game engine, so it feels alive.

Vision

A second brain you own, end to end

Local-first

Your notes and your database live in your user folder, on your disk. No account and no Docker. AI features use your own OpenAI key, stored in your system's credential store, and send only what each request needs.

Immersive

Built with a game engine. Your ideas form a living constellation, thoughts bloom when you capture them, and each theme (Morning, Focus, Evening) has its own music and sounds.

Transparent

The engine room shows what really runs, with real measurements only. Everything the assistant changes is journaled and can be undone, and every AI call shows its estimated cost.

How it works

From clone to a running system in a few clicks

  1. 1

    Open the project in Godot

    Run it. The welcome screen reads the install state from disk.

  2. 2

    Press Setup

    Godot finds a compatible Python (3.12 or 3.13) and starts the bootstrap without freezing the interface.

  3. 3

    Watch the real installation

    Virtual environment, hash-locked dependencies, SQLite migrations. Each step streams structured events, progress and logs.

  4. 4

    Talk to your brain

    Type or speak a thought: it blooms in the graph and the assistant files it in the right project. Each morning she reads you a briefing. Ask her anything and she answers from your notes.

Screenshots captured from the real application with the real assistant (Loops 3 and 4), on demo data.

Features roadmap

What works, what is coming, what is an idea

Implemented

  • Assistant: spoken morning briefing, answers with citations, acts on your notes
  • Every capture filed automatically, with undo
  • Areas, projects and next actions, a Today view, checklists

Open-source philosophy

Readable, inspectable, yours to change

Second Brain GD is developed in the open spirit: plain formats, a documented event protocol, and an engine you can read in an afternoon. Nothing is hidden behind a service. Metrics that cannot be measured say “Not available” instead of guessing.

The repository is private while the foundations settle. The license will be chosen before the first public release.

Development journal

Built in loops

Loop 5b November 3, 2026

A memory that understands meaning

Every note is now indexed by meaning, quietly in the background. Ask « c'est quoi mes habitudes pour mieux dormir? » and the assistant finds your English note “Bedtime routine”, though no word matches. Notes about the same idea now find each other: they are joined in the graph by a warm dashed line, and listed as related ideas when you open one. Measured on real notes: three true pairs found, no false ones.

Loop 6a November 2, 2026

Project mode

Open a project and the assistant works inside it: she knows its goal, due date and next actions, searches the project first, and follows the project's own instructions. Tell a client project “English-speaking client, contact Mark Chen” and the follow-up email comes out in English, addressed to Mark.

Loop 5 November 2, 2026

Sources: files and links

Drop a PDF or a Word file on the window, or paste a link: the brain reads it, writes a short summary card into your graph and indexes every passage. Ask the assistant and she answers from your documents, with the source cited.

Loop 4 November 2, 2026

Structure: areas, projects, today

Your life domains (family, health, home, vision at work) now hold projects with a goal, a due date and next actions. A Today view shows what is late, due and next, plus your lists. Say « faut acheter du lait pis du café » and it lands on the grocery list. Once a week, the assistant walks you through every project and asks the questions that move your vision forward.

Loop 3 November 2, 2026

An assistant who talks back

The assistant arrives. At the first opening of the day she reads a short briefing aloud: open tasks, what is new, an idea that has been sleeping. She answers from your notes and cites them, saves or moves notes when you ask, and files every capture in the right project. Everything she changes can be undone. You can type to her or speak to her.

Loop 2 November 2, 2026

Voice capture

Press the microphone and your words appear while you speak, then bloom as a note. Each user brings their own OpenAI key during onboarding. It is stored in the system's credential store and never by the interface. Measured on the real API: English transcribed without errors, first words in about a second.

Loop 1 November 2, 2026

Immersion and the knowledge core

The second brain itself arrives: a living graph where captured thoughts bloom, spaces for projects, [[links]], #tags, full-text search and a morning briefing. A soft blue Morning theme replaces the green, with Focus, Evening and Forest alongside. Each theme has generated music, ambience and interface sounds.

Loop 0 November 2, 2026

Foundation, bootstrap and System Console

The first increment works end to end: Godot finds Python, a standard-library bootstrap builds the environment, migrates SQLite and starts a local FastAPI service, and the System Console shows live, measured state. Tested headless on Linux (Python 3.12) and native Windows (Microsoft Store Python 3.13), including a fix for the Store's silent AppData redirection.
